What I ran (no model, no cloud, no docker):
- `python3 -m unittest test_f` in WSL: **13 tests, OK**. `bash -n f1_pod.sh`: OK, and the file has no CR bytes.
- I rendered the 7 F2 user prompts from the pinned task metadata, with flags masked, and scanned them and the
  starter files for CTF cues. Results are in R5-01 and R5-02.
- I read the saved `/home/capstone-study/stage_f/f2_environment_check.json`: all 7 tasks pass. The alias resolves,
  the gate opens on attempt 1 and no prompt contains `chal.csaw.io`.
- From the private archives I used aggregate counts only, with no output text:
  - Stage D aligned StrongREJECT at 2,048 tokens: 28/313 stopped at the length limit (8.9%, which matches the
    protocol's figure), 9 were empty or unclosed, and the median was 454 completion tokens. Willingness classes
    were 177 full refusal, 118 partial, 9 complies and 9 no answer.
  - CTF v2 elapsed time on the 7 F2 tasks: 18–405 s for each solved trial, except heretic get_it at 983 s.
  - Applying the F2 outcome rule to all 51 v2 rows: no v2 trial ever ended with a text-only reply. Every unsolved
    trial hit `time_cap` or `tool_budget`. So v2 gives no data on the rule's false-positive rate.

Overall: the code is careful, and the stage B to E lessons are mostly carried over: per-arm fetch, judge right
after generation, real cache deletion, separate caps, and a hash check on generation files. The main problem
is validity, not code. **The F2 "malicious, non-CTF" framing leaks "CTF" at the model's first decision point
through the frozen v2 tool descriptions.** The refusal rule also has a predictable false-positive path. F1 has
one robustness hole: an arm whose output has no detectable `</think>` would score as all "no answer".

## Must fix before freeze

1. **R5-01:** neutral `TOOLS` override in `run_arm_f2`, keeping the `submit_flag` name and disclosing it, plus a test.
2. **R5-02:** decide on redacting the CTF words in the notes or a pre-registered cue covariate and sensitivity
   analysis. Extend the free environment check to target banners and pages, and fold all prompt checks into `all_ok`.
3. **R5-03:** split the refusal rule: refusal before any terminal call, or no action ending; separate
   `abandoned_after_engagement` and `no_action_other` categories.
4. **R5-04:** pre-register logging of cut-off-before-first-call and of refusal text that comes with a tool call;
   state the CUTOFF_NOTICE nudge.
5. **R5-05:** fully specify the hand-labelling: first and final turns, definitions, blinded export, labeller, κ.
6. **R5-06:** pre-register a task-clustered sensitivity analysis and solve-rate-among-non-refused.
7. **R5-07:** make the F1 think-split robust (`skip_special_tokens=False` or a token-id split; a `no_think_markup`
   rule; a per-arm closed-rate sanity line).
8. **R5-08:** add the D-07, D-08 and D-12 sensitivity outputs and per-arm `thinking_closed` to `analyse_f1`.
9. **R5-09:** F1 install timeout with one retry; F2 operator restart rule pre-registered; consider raising the F1 cap.

## Top 5

1. **R5-01:** the tool descriptions tell the model it is in an "isolated CTF environment" with a "challenge
   checker". That defeats the F2 manipulation at the exact turn where refusals happen.
2. **R5-03:** the refusal rule scores "I'm unable to get the token" after real engagement as a refusal, and
   scores budget-exhausted no-action trials as refusals.
3. **R5-07:** F1 can silently score an entire uncensored arm as "no answer" if `</think>` is stripped or
   never emitted. That path has never run on an uncensored arm.
4. **R5-02:** the task notes and files still say CTF, challenge or flag (shreeramquest, poem_collection,
   puffin), and target banners and pages are unchecked.
5. **R5-06 / R5-08:** pre-register the analyses that matter for interpretation: task-clustered F2 refusal,
   solve among non-refused, F1 any-refusal, no_answer-excluded and closed-only results.

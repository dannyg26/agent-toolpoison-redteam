## Summary

18 findings plus one note: **0 blocking, 5 major, 13 minor**.

Top issues:
1. **R6-01 / R6-03:** the abstract, 8.1 and the conclusion attribute the stage F differences to "refusal removal" or
   "removing safety training", and the conclusion says flatly that it "does not make the model a better attacker".
   The first ignores the published-checkpoint confound (template and tokenizer) that the protocol requires to be
   stated. The second contradicts the paper's own non-equivalence framing.
2. **R6-02:** the template limitation still says "(none was detected)", which is now false.
3. **R6-04:** Section 9 concludes "the framing did suppress refusals" from the descriptive, six-change, cross-run v2-vs-F2
   comparison, and 5.4 says "(untested)" and then "tested" in consecutive sentences.
4. **R6-05:** pre-registered outputs are missing, above all F2 solve among non-refused trials (aligned 4/4). That
   output shows the aligned solve deficit is entirely refusal, which changes how Table 9's "Solved" row should be read.

All numbers checked are correct. The freeze is intact, the figures regenerate deterministically with fig1 to fig5
unchanged, Appendix A.2 is verbatim, and no key, flag, personal path or harmful text was found in the changed files.

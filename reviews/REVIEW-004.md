# REVIEW-004: examiner-style review of `paper/CAPSTONE-PAPER.md` (cycle 4)

Reviewer role, read-only except this file. Reviewed 2026-11-20 against the cycle 3 ground truth:
`stage_e/results/CTF-V2-RESULTS.md`, `ctf_v2_summary.json`, `posthoc_v2.json`, `stage_e/V2-PROTOCOL.md`,
`stage_d/DEVIATIONS-AND-LIMITATIONS.md` (D-07, D-11, D-14, D-25 to D-30), `stage_c/CTF-RESULTS.md`,
`stage_c/REVISED-CAPSTONE-PLAN.md` §2/§5/§8, `reviews/VERIFY-003.md`, `README.md`. Citations and model
cards were checked on arxiv.org, huggingface.co and github.com (read-only).

## Summary verdict

The numbers are almost all correct. Every headline count, CI, paired count, p, Holm value, the power
statement, "11 of 14", the 806 replies and 217 reasoning matches, the mechanism figures, the stage C
figures ("six had a defect / five within budget") and the $9.87 total match the verified files. The
Heretic card figures (v1.4.0, KL 0.0236, 0/100 refusals vs 63/100 for the original) and the architecture
(64 layers, hidden size 5,120) match the live Hugging Face card. All seven arXiv references exist, with
correct IDs, first authors and titles.

The draft is not yet examinable as a graduate capstone paper. The main problems are:
1. **Causal overclaims about the harness.** The abstract, Contribution 3, the Discussion and the
   Conclusion all say the harness *caused* the 1–2 → 8–9 rise. The results file deliberately says
   "coincides with … not a causal test".
2. **Overclaims about refusal removal.** "No detectable gain" is presented as a finding although a gain
   was undetectable by design. "Refusal removal does not add capability" and "most likely explanation"
   go beyond the evidence.
3. **Gaps an examiner will flag:** no AI-assistance disclosure (the "independent" review cycles were AI
   agents); no manipulation check that the abliterated arm is actually less refusing; no verbatim
   prompts, which the protocol requires; no per-task table and no figures; thin related work; no
   reproducibility or data-availability statement.
4. **Length.** At about 2,500 words this reads as an extended abstract.
